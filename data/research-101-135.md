## Identity and canonical handling

- Reused game/movie/show entities retain existing English canonical titles and existing part/year metadata when an exact existing entity was available. Examples include Minecraft, Mario Kart 8/Deluxe, Toy Story, Ratatouille, WALL-E and Avatar: The Last Airbender.
- Character identities are scoped by domain to prevent names such as Batman, Spider-Man or Cinderella from colliding with film/franchise titles.
- Repeated new anime identities use the same English names across categories: Son Goku, Conan Edogawa, Gon Freecss, Naruto Uzumaki, Monkey D. Luffy and Tanjiro Kamado.
- Existing unambiguous film canonical forms were retained for The Little Mermaid and Minions. New year-qualified aliases remain accepted.
- **Known old-catalog ambiguity:** old distractor entries use the bare title `The Lion King` for different references/versions. New category119 intentionally keeps `The Lion King (1994)` with year1994 and also a generic-name alias, so a bare title can trigger clarification rather than conflating the 1994 animation with the 2019 film. No old files were changed.
- Existing old distractors sometimes omit series/year fields even where the title itself contains a part/year. Those older omissions are preserved rather than silently changing old identities in this task.
