## Meaning of the positions

All 30 lists carry rankingType=editorial, difficulty=easy, and region=global. Slots 1–10 were deliberately arranged for this game; no popularity survey, objective “best ten,” scientific rank, or publisher endorsement is implied. The visible metric follows the requested positive wording: المراكز مرتبة خصيصًا لهذا التحدّي؛ المرجع يوضح الأسماء ومعلوماتها.

Reference URLs substantiate the identities and topic membership, while the game owns selection and position. A reference may itself contain a statistical or ranked table (footballers, attendance, ESPN), but that source order is not the order of these challenges.

Direct primary/reference publishers additionally used: JNTO for Japanese dishes, UEFA's historical club directory, LA28's official sports directory, the Natural History Museum's dinosaur directory, and ESPN's athlete profiles/article.

The JNTO page uses dynamic content, and ESPN's full article triggered a JavaScript verification screen in the browsing tool. Their indexed primary-source material and supplementary encyclopedia/official references were used for identity checks; this does not affect any game position because positions are editorial.

## Theme-park status and identity (195)

The selected ten are existing, operating parks, not planned parks or retired names. Owner sources checked:
- Disney Experiences' current destination directory: https://disneyexperiences.com/parks/ — Disneyland Resort, Walt Disney World, Tokyo Disney Resort, Disneyland Paris, Shanghai Disney Resort, Hong Kong Disneyland.
- Disneyland Paris' November 2026 park calendar and its current Disneyland Park page: https://www.disneylandparis.com/en-gb/calendars/park-hours and https://www.disneylandparis.com/en-int/destinations/disneyland-park.
- Universal's current destination directory: https://corporate.universaldestinationsandexperiences.com/destinations-experiences/ — Hollywood, Orlando and Japan.
- Universal Studios Florida's own page displayed “Open Today” and November 2026–January 2027 events: https://www.universalorlando.com/web/en/us/theme-parks/universal-studios-florida.
- Europa-Park's own 2026–2027 tickets page confirms the operating season: https://www.europapark.de/en/theme-park/tickets-offers.
- The cited encyclopedia attendance table contains all ten park identities. It is used as an identity catalog here, not a transferred attendance ranking.
- Location-based series/part fields distinguish Disneyland Anaheim, Tokyo, Paris, Hong Kong and Shanghai, and Universal Hollywood, Florida and Japan. These part values are city/location names, not numerals.
