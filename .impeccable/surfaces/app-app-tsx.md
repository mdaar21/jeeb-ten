# Surface brief: ألعاب الجلسة (hub + جيب العشرة + خمن الموقع)

Scope: whole site, mode Experience on the hub (the games themselves are Operate-like: scanability first).
Audience: families and friends in evening sessions, one shared screen, readable from a distance.
Open decisions: none.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a street of lit shopfronts at night. Each game is a shop with its own hand-painted sign; each category is a sign board. Refuses the dark-teal "gaming app" with glowing mint accents and rounded identical cards.
OWN-WORLD: Cobalt painted wall (#1c3fc4 family) as the ground. Sign boards in enamel white, saffron, signal red, leaf green, sky. Ink-navy outlines and double keylines, soft real drop shadows, striped awnings from repeating-linear-gradient, a row of marquee bulbs on the main fascia. Display in Lalezar (sign-painter Arabic), UI in Almarai. No gradients on text, no glow halos.
STORY: A family arrives, sees two shops, walks into one in a single tap, and plays in a minute. Success: the first round starts in under 30 seconds.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width fascia sign "ألعاب الجلسة" with marquee bulbs at top; below it two large shopfronts side by side (stacked on phone), each with striped awning, painted game name at display scale, a live preview window (rank slots / globe) and a single bold enter button. Primary action is each shop's door button.
FORM: Seed reference seed:ee3eef8274f56d38, grounded candidate 4 of 7 (popular hand-painted shop sign), roll degraded (no catalog).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
